Who We Are

We rethought car rental. From scratch.

SpotlessRide was founded in 2016 by a team of travel enthusiasts frustrated by outdated rental companies — hidden fees, long queues, and vehicles that felt like an afterthought.

We set out to build something completely different: a digital-first rental experience that puts the customer in control. No paperwork. No surprises. Just the keys and the open road.

Today, SpotlessRide operates across South Africa with over 1,200 vehicles and a community of more than 50,000 loyal drivers. But our mission hasn't changed — make every journey feel like it was built just for you.

Our Journey

How we got here

From a startup with 12 cars to South Africa's most-loved rental platform.

2016

FlexRide is born

Launched in Johannesburg with 12 vehicles and a big dream. First 100 customers served in month one.

2018

Mobile app launch

Our iOS and Android app goes live, enabling fully paperless booking for the first time in SA.

2020

Airport transfer network

Expanded to 14 major airports across South Africa. 50,000 airport trips completed in year one.

2022

Subscription plans introduced

Launched FlexRide Monthly — the first car subscription service with unlimited vehicle swaps in Africa.

2024

EV fleet expansion

Added 200 electric vehicles across our fleet. Committed to 50% EV penetration by 2027.
