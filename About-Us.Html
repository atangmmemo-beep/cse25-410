Who We Are

We rethought car washing. From scratch.

SpotlessShine was founded in 2018 by a team of car enthusiasts frustrated by rushed, low-quality washes — vehicles returned damp, streaky, and no cleaner than before.

We set out to build something completely different: a professional-grade wash experience that puts quality first. No shortcuts. No water spots. Just a gleaming, spotless finish every single time.

Today, SpotlessShine operates across Botswanawith 15 premium wash bays and over 80,000 loyal customers. But our mission hasn't changed — make every car look like it just rolled out of the showroom.

What Drives Us

Our core values

Every product we use, every process we follow, every team member we hire — it all comes back to these four principles.

Customer First

Every service, every process, and every hire is shaped around making your experience better. Full stop.

Spotless Standards

No missed spots. No hidden charges. Every wash is inspected before handover — if it's not perfect, we redo it free of charge.

Eco-Conscious

We use biodegradable products and water-recycling systems at every location. A clean car shouldn't cost the planet.

Always Improving

We train constantly, invest in new techniques, and listen to every review. Your feedback shapes every service update we make.

Our Journey

How we got here

From a single wash bay to Botswana,s most-loved car wash brand.

2018

SpotlessShine is born

Opened our first wash bay in Gaborone with a team of 5. First 500 customers served in month one.

2019

Premium detailing suite launched

Added interior detailing and paint correction services — a first for the Gaborone area.

2021

Booking app goes live

Our iOS and Android app launches, enabling fully cashless, queue-free booking across all locations.

2022

Wash subscription plans introduced

Launched SpotlessRide Monthly — the first unlimited car wash subscription service in Botswana.

2024

Eco wash system rollout

Installed water-recycling rigs at all 15 locations, cutting water usage by 70%. Committed to full sustainability by 2026.
