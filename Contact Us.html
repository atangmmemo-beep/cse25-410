Frequently Asked Questions

You'll need a valid driver's licence, a credit or debit card in your name, and a government-issued ID. International renters should also bring their passport and an International Driving Permit if applicable.

Log in to your account, navigate to "My Bookings", and select the reservation you'd like to change. Cancellations and modifications are free up to 48 hours before pickup. Changes within 48 hours may incur a small fee.

Vehicles are provided with a full tank. We ask that you return the car with a full tank. If the vehicle is returned with less fuel, a refuelling fee will be charged based on the amount needed plus a service charge.

A grace period of 30 minutes applies. Returns between 30 minutes and 3 hours late are charged a half-day rate. Returns more than 3 hours late are charged a full-day rate. We recommend extending your booking in advance via the app to avoid this.

Yes — cross-border travel is available to selected neighbouring countries including Namibia, Botswana, Lesotho, Eswatini, and Mozambique. A cross-border letter and additional fee apply. Please inform us at the time of booking.
